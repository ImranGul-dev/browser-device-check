## Start with a complete browser check

Run the [Complete Device Check](/device-check/) before opening the interview platform. Complete the camera, microphone, speaker, permission, browser, display, and screen-sharing steps. Treat **Ready with warnings** as a prompt to investigate, not as a score.

## Camera checklist

- Confirm that the intended camera appears and shows a live moving image.
- Open a physical privacy shutter or remove a lens cover.
- Clean the lens according to manufacturer guidance.
- Place light in front of you rather than directly behind you.
- Keep the camera near eye level and use a stable surface.
- Check that the background does not reveal confidential or distracting information.
- Close applications that may already be using the camera.

The browser preview is useful evidence, but it does not grade professional image quality or guarantee that the interview platform will select the same camera.

## Microphone checklist

- Confirm that normal speech creates a changing waveform and relative level.
- Check physical mute buttons and headset controls.
- Choose the intended input in both the browser test and interview platform.
- Position the microphone consistently and avoid speaking directly into the capsule.
- Listen for fans, traffic, keyboard noise, and other continuing activity.
- Charge Bluetooth devices and keep a wired fallback when possible.

The microphone test reports relative browser activity, not calibrated decibels or a professional acoustic score.

## Speaker and headphone checklist

Use a low comfortable volume. Play the left, right, stereo, speech, and centered samples. Confirm what you heard because the page does not use a microphone to verify output automatically.

Check operating-system output selection, balance, mono-audio settings, cable or adapter connections, and Bluetooth routing. Keep the **Stop Sound** control available.

## Browser and platform preparation

1. Use a current supported browser.
2. Sign in to the interview platform early when an account is required there.
3. Review the platform’s camera, microphone, and speaker selectors.
4. Use the platform’s official preview, test meeting, or device settings after the browser tests.
5. Confirm whether screen sharing will be required and whether organization policy permits it.
6. Close unnecessary tabs and applications.

## Lighting and framing

Face a window or diffused light when practical. Avoid a bright window directly behind you. Keep your eyes near the upper third of the frame, leave a small amount of space above your head, and position the camera far enough away to avoid a distorted close-up.

Approximate brightness warnings from a browser preview are not calibrated lighting analysis. Use your own visual review.

## Connection and power

- Connect the computer to power or confirm a healthy battery.
- Prefer a stable wired or strong Wi-Fi connection.
- Pause large downloads, backups, and updates.
- Keep the router and device in a stable location.
- Have the interview link and contact information available separately.

This site does not run a network-speed certification. Use the platform’s official network guidance when bandwidth is a concern.

## Five-minute review

Five minutes before the interview:

1. Reopen the platform preview.
2. Confirm the selected camera, microphone, and speaker.
3. Check framing, lighting, volume, and mute state.
4. Close notification panels and unrelated applications.
5. Place the device on power.
6. Keep water, notes, and any permitted documents within reach.
7. Join early enough to resolve an unexpected permission prompt.

## Backup plan

Prepare one practical alternative:

- A wired headset if Bluetooth routing fails.
- A second browser when the platform supports it.
- A phone or second device with the platform installed.
- The interviewer’s contact method in case the meeting link fails.
- A nearby charging cable.

Do not join simultaneously from two devices with active microphones and speakers unless one is muted, because feedback can occur.

## What a browser check cannot guarantee

A successful browser result does not guarantee an interview outcome, platform compatibility, organization policy, network stability, or identical device selection. It provides a structured way to reduce preventable technical problems.
