## Run the webcam test first

Open the [Webcam Test](/webcam-test/) and note the first clear symptom:

- Permission blocked or no prompt.
- No camera available.
- Camera may be in use.
- A black preview.
- A frozen preview.
- The wrong camera.
- Blur, darkness, excessive brightness, or unusual proportions.

Change one setting at a time and rerun the test so you can see which action changed the result.

## Permission blocked

Open the browser’s site-information control, reset Camera permission, reload, and select **Allow Camera** again. Then check operating-system privacy settings. A browser setting and an operating-system setting can disagree.

On managed devices, an administrator may enforce the decision. Try another current browser only after checking policy and support requirements.

## No camera available

1. Confirm that an external camera is connected and powered.
2. Open any physical privacy switch.
3. Reconnect the device directly rather than through an unreliable hub.
4. Restart the browser.
5. Check the operating system’s camera application.
6. Restart the device if the camera remains absent everywhere.

If the camera is missing from the operating system as well as the browser, manufacturer support or professional service may be needed.

## Camera may be in use

Close meeting, recording, streaming, camera, and browser-tab sessions. Some applications keep a device active after their visible window closes. Quit them fully, then reload the test.

Disconnecting a camera while another application is using it can leave a stale state until the application or browser restarts.

## Black preview

- Open a privacy shutter or lens cover.
- Increase light in front of the camera.
- Choose another camera from the selector.
- Remove temporary protective film.
- Close competing applications.
- Disconnect and reconnect an external camera.
- Compare the camera in the operating-system camera application.

A camera indicator light can turn on even when the lens is blocked or the scene is too dark.

## Frozen preview

Move visibly in front of the camera and compare the image. If it does not change, stop and restart the test. Close other camera applications, reconnect an external camera, lower competing system load, and test another USB port.

The page does not declare hardware failure after a single unchanged frame. Browser scheduling, a paused track, or another application can cause similar behavior.

## Wrong camera selected

Permission may be required before meaningful device labels appear. Open **Choose Another Camera**, select each available label, and compare the preview. Use the same label inside the meeting application.

Virtual cameras and capture software can appear beside physical devices. Disable them temporarily when they create confusion.

## Blurry, dark, bright, or stretched image

Clean the lens safely and remove protective film. Improve front lighting and reduce strong backlighting. Keep the camera stable and within its focus range. Check whether browser zoom, page layout, or another application is stretching the displayed image.

Browser-delivered width, height, aspect ratio, and frame rate are not professional quality measurements.

## Works here but not in another application

1. Open that application’s video settings.
2. Select the same camera that worked in the browser.
3. Check the application’s operating-system camera permission.
4. Use the platform’s official preview or test meeting.
5. Sign in with the correct account.
6. Contact an administrator when organization policy restricts devices.

## When to seek additional help

Compare the camera in another browser, the operating-system camera application, another USB port, and another device when possible. Persistent absence, physical damage, overheating, cable faults, or manufacturer error messages may require qualified support.

The browser test is practical evidence, not hardware certification or warranty determination.
